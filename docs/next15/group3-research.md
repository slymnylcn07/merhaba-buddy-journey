## Editorial and evidence rules

All factual medical guidance was checked against original studies or primary issuing medical organizations. Six or more such sources are present in every article.sources list. Golf additionally includes one clearly supplemental systematic review. Research limitations are reflected in the prose. No diagnosis or cure promises, no self-unlocking, no provocative self-tests, no manual CTA or source boxes, and no em dashes. Educational photographs do not demonstrate injury-specific treatment. The Hoffa model is a simplified anatomical illustration, not a scan or representation of disease.

Web verification used direct opens and indexed primary-source abstracts/full text. Some later direct PMC/BMJ opens returned a browser challenge or 403; the corresponding indexed original study text or PubMed abstract was available and checked. No challenge was bypassed. URLs retained below are the original source destinations, not search pages.

## golf-knee-pain: source evidence

| Primary source | Use and limit |
|---|---|
| [Lower Body Joint Moments during the Golf Swing in Older Adults: Comparison to Other Activities of Daily Living](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10499118/) (Journal of Sports Science and Medicine) | Joint moments compared golf with walking and sit-to-stand in 22 healthy men. Biomechanical differences do not establish tissue diagnosis or a safe swing count. |
| [Three-dimensional kinematics in healthy older adult males during golf swings](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7044058/) (Sports Biomechanics) | Older male kinematic study. Do not generalize to all ages, painful knees, or a universal correct swing. |
| [Potential biomechanical risk factors on developing lead knee osteoarthritis in the golf swing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9805371/) (Scientific Reports) | Observational biomechanical associations with lead-knee loading. Not proof that a stance change prevents osteoarthritis. |
| [Tibiofemoral axial rotation during the golf swing is influenced by total knee arthroplasty bearing type and foot rotation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12690408/) (Journal of Experimental Orthopaedics) | Small in-vivo arthroplasty biomechanics study (11 participants). Bearing type and foot rotation interact; not a generic natural-knee prescription. |
| [Knee pain](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/knee-pain/) (NHS) | Urgency, painful clicking versus painless noise, and advice against self-diagnosis. |
| [Meniscus Tears](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/meniscus-tears/) (American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons) | Possible meniscal symptoms overlap other problems; symptoms alone cannot diagnose a tear. |

Nerve fibers support sensitivity; not a diagnostic threshold. |
| [Ultrasound evaluation of infrapatellar fat pad impingement: An exploratory prospective study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29525550/) (The Knee) | Exploratory ultrasound study of 11 symptomatic and 10 asymptomatic knees. No claim that ultrasound is a definitive simple test. |
| [Knee pain](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/knee-pain/) (NHS) | Urgent knee warning signs. Persistent movement block is not presumed Hoffa irritation. |

## Product mapping and safeguards

Main owns product mapping. Appropriate editorial boundary for all five: products are optional comfort/support tools only after suitability is established, never treatments for a mechanical block, unexplained swelling, suspected fracture, syndesmotic injury, dislocation, or new neurological/circulation symptoms. Golf and hypermobile content links the existing brace-versus-sleeve explanation without promising protection. Hoffa discourages deep pressure or attempts to release the pad. Locking and ankle-injury guides explicitly prohibit using a device to delay assessment. No product is presented as the solution to an injured or locked knee.
