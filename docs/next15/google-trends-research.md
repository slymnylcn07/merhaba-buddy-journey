## Interpretation

All 15 topics were checked. Broad seeds are language-discovery aids, not proof that a knee-specific phrase is popular. These observations are not inserted as growth claims into consumer-facing medical articles. No access to fresh Search Console or Bing query exports was assumed for this batch. Primary-source research and exact cannibalization boundaries are recorded in the group research files and content plan.
