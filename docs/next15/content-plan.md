## Evidence rules

- Google Trends is relative interest, not search volume, and related-query growth does not prove growth for a knee-pain long tail.
- Filter unrelated brand, recall, supplement and software queries even when Trends labels them rising.
- Sources and actual observed Trends findings are recorded separately. Do not insert SEO-research claims into reader-facing medical copy.
- New clinical pages must not imply a diagnosis from a diagram, MRI finding, or online self-test.
- Existing pages receive narrow editorial crosslinks only; no slug changes, redirects, title replacement or broad body rewrite.

## Integration checklist

- Article loaders and recent guide metadata, published/modified dates and reading time.
- Thumbnail loaders AND the Guides page thumbnail map, category membership, related-guide links.
- Unique article-specific CTA copy and explicit product override, avoiding default heat recommendations for injury pages.
- Reverse links from established relevant guides; all destinations canonical.
- Source/content/asset checks, rendered word/image/FAQ checks, lint, full production build and representative responsive visual QA.
